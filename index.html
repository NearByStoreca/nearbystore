How it works

Three steps, about ten seconds.

  1. 1

    Snap the receipt

    Point your camera at any printed receipt. Crumpled, faded, and angled shots are fine.

  2. 2

    We read every line

    Our scanner pulls out each item, quantity, and price, and matches them against what you've bought before.

  3. 3

    See your spending clearly

    Track where your money goes each month, and watch how prices move on the things you buy regularly.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it cost anything?

Scanning receipts and tracking your spending is free. Placeholder copy — replace with your real pricing once decided.

Which stores does it work with?

Any store that prints a receipt — the scanner reads the paper, so it doesn't depend on a partnership. Comparing prices between stores is still in development.

What happens to my receipt photos?

They're stored against your account so you can search them later. Payment card details are never retained.

What if the scanner reads something wrong?

You can correct any line item yourself, and low-confidence reads are flagged for review before they affect prices.
